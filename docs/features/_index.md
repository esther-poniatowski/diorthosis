# Features

Each feature of diorthosis is specified in one note, by the format read or written by the plugin, and by the behaviour of the plugin. Each note states the target behaviour of its feature, whereas the steps already implemented are checked in the [roadmap](../../TODO.md). The notes are listed in dependency order.

- [Review records](review-records.md): to write or read the record of a review item, and to predict where the plugin files the record.
- [Selectors](selectors.md): to designate the scope of an item in its targets, and to predict how the scope is recovered after an edit.
- [Review interface](review-interface.md): to raise, read, answer and close items beside the open file, and to find every open item of the vault.
